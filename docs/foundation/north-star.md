## The soul, in one sentence

**Pick your style and theme once; your channel writes, renders, posts and
learns by itself while you sleep.**

## The five-second promise

**It posted while I slept, and I can see what worked.** You open UGC in the
morning: last night's videos are live, this morning's are queued, and the
numbers already say which hook won. Calm command, not a content treadmill.

## What "insanely great" means here

1. **Set once, runs forever.** A channel is configured in minutes and then
   needs nothing but an occasional approval.
2. **Every video looks like the channel.** Style and theme hold across hundreds
   of videos; nothing reads as generic AI filler.
3. **Honest numbers.** Metrics come from the platforms, are labelled with when
   they were taken, and never get padded.
4. **Never silent failure.** A failed render, a missing account or empty
   credits shows up as a plain sentence with a fix, not a stuck spinner.
